Thiết kế cơ chế queueing/throttling giúp hệ thống hoạt động ổn định, tránh đứt gãy luồng đồng bộ khi xử lý cửa hàng có hàng chục nghìn SKU.

  • Entity Relationship & Domain Analysis: Thoroughly analyzed official API documentation for Shopify and TikTok Ads Manager. Mapped complex entity relationships across Shopify Stores, TikTok Business Center (BC), TikTok Ad Accounts, TikTok Pixels, and Product Catalogs.

  • Product Field Mapping Logic: Engineered transformation rules mapping Shopify product attributes to TikTok Catalog standards (SKUs, titles, pricing, variants, availability, taxonomy). Built error-handling workflows for missing required attributes and disapproved item status.
  • Performance Optimization & Rate Limit Mitigation: Evaluated API rate limits for both platforms. Architected a hybrid sync strategy utilizing Shopify Bulk Operations API for scheduled mass data ingestion alongside real-time Webhook triggers for updates. Designed a queuing and throttling architecture to maintain system stability when syncing stores with tens of thousands of SKUs.
